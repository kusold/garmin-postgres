# Garmin archive and Notion sync

Terms for the archived Garmin data and its per-user Notion destination.

## Language

**Activity archive run**:
One attempt to archive a Garmin activity for a user, including its activity row and any selected detail or original file.

**Notion sync target**:
The Notion destination configured for one Garmin user, including its integration token and database IDs.

**Notion sync run**:
One attempt to sync selected archived Garmin data for one active user to that user's Notion sync target.

**Personal-record snapshot**:
The user's full archived personal-record history considered together when updating Notion, independent of an activity or daily-steps date window.
